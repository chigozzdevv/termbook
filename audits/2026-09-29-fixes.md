## Recovery and monitoring

- Cure requires an actual verified payment after recovery starts. Partial app recovery must repay recovered free cash and improve current health atomically.
- The risk monitor reads current accrued debt and authoritative backing at one finalized block. It advances default, reviewed app recovery, cash repayment and remote seizure; it reports required source proof, collateral bids and House finalization.
- Destination allocation and source custody clearance use separate Wormhole domains from route registration. Source delivery confirmation preserves late refundable claims. SOL clearance verifies held balance, latest seizure and funding/refund counters.
- Recovery inbox completion requires empty custody and allocated minimum proceeds. Above-cap funds can repay debt separately. Expired SOL operations can retire against verified, unused refunds covering their full original funding amount. Partial refund attribution and unrecovered routing losses require further policy and reconciliation work.
- Ethereum authorizations discovered by the finalized indexer enter the durable relay queue. A caught-up indexer refreshes readiness even when no new block arrives.
- Keeper transactions persist raw signatures before broadcast, use wallet leases, reconcile canonical finality independently of original action eligibility, retain fee-replacement candidates and allow a simulated retry after a finalized revert. Replacements preserve the exact authorized transaction fields.
- A repeated SOL return checks the recorded source action and emits a fresh checkpoint instead of executing that action again. SDK HTTP errors preserve retryable protocol codes; account health uses current backing.
